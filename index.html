How to test
  1. Enter your UPI ID and shop name, then add your workers.
  2. Scan one QR with GPay, PhonePe or Paytm and pay ₹1.
  3. Open the payment in your own UPI app and check that the worker's name shows in the note.
  4. If a worker has their own UPI ID, enter it on their card. Their QR will pay that ID instead.
